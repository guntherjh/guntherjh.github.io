# Biome over ESLint + Prettier

We replaced ESLint 10 (flat config, `eslint:recommended` only, no plugins) and Prettier 3 (`tabWidth: 4`) with [Biome](https://biomejs.dev/), a single Rust-based tool covering both linting and formatting for JS, JSON, and CSS. This trades ESLint's larger rule/plugin ecosystem and both tools' longer-established governance (ESLint: OpenJS Foundation; Prettier: long-standing, near-universal adoption) for one dependency instead of four, one config file instead of two, and no more `eslint-config-prettier` shim to keep the two tools from fighting over formatting — a real simplification for a repo this size, at the cost of betting on Biome's newer, differently-governed project (a community fork of the discontinued Rome Tools, sponsored by Depot; ~3 years of stable releases as of this decision). Migrating also means accepting Biome's default tab indentation in place of the repo's prior 4-space convention, and losing lint coverage for a small number (~8-9 of 64) of `eslint:recommended` rules with no Biome equivalent — edge cases (e.g. `no-invalid-regexp`, `no-delete-var`) this codebase has no history of tripping. Full research backing this decision, with primary-source citations: [docs/research/biome-migration.md](../research/biome-migration.md) (on the `research/biome-migration` branch). guntherjh/guntherjh.github.io#37.
